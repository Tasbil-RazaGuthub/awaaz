# Code of Conduct

Awaaz exists to make health support easier to reach for people who are often overlooked. That starts with how we treat each other in this project.

## Our standard

- Be respectful, especially in disagreement. Critique ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith. Most misunderstandings are just that.
- No harassment, discriminatory language, or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests, or discussions.
- Remember the people this project is for. If a suggestion would make the app less safe, less private, or less usable for a migrant worker with a low-end phone and limited data, that's a valid reason to push back on it, kindly.

## Scope

This applies to every space connected to the project: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any chat or forum where people gather as part of Awaaz.

## Reporting

If something crosses a line, open a private message to a maintainer rather than a public issue. Reports are handled quietly and taken seriously.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and comments that don't fit this standard, and may temporarily or permanently block anyone whose behavior is harmful to the community.

This is a short, plain-language code of conduct rather than a legal document, adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
